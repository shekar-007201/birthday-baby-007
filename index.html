Your Story Arc

Our first Meeting

Naa love story degree days lo start kaaledu… school days lone start ayyindi. First time thanani Miryalagudem lo kalisa. Appatnunchi thanante naku chala istam. Kani adi complete ga one-side love. Naku thanante chala istam unna… thanu matram appudu natho pedda ga matladedi kadu. Nenu kuda thanani dooram nunchi chusthu, aa feelings ni naalone dachukunevadini. School days ala aipoyayi. Tarvatha degree ki vachaka malli maa madhya connection vachindi. Appudu memu chatting, calls cheskuntu chala close ayyamu. Time ela pass ayyedho kuda telisedi kadu. Appudu nenu naa feelings thanaki cheppesa… propose chesa. Ame kuda nannu accept chesindi. ❤️ Appati varaku one-side ga unna naa love… aa moment tho iddari love story ga maarindi.


Part 2

Thanani first lo nenu istapaddanu… Kani konni rojulu tarvatha tanu naaku oka habit aipoyindi. Roju thantho matladakunda undalekapoyevadini. Chat aina, call aina… thantho matladatam naa daily life lo oka part aipoyindi. Maa madhya pedda godavalu em undevu kadu. Kani thanaki kopam konchem ekkuva. Chinna chinna things ki arguments ayyina moments undevi. Kani entha kopam vachina, entha godava aina… tanu nannu vadilesedi kadu. Nenu kuda thanani vadulukoledu. Appude naaku ardham ayyindi… Tanu naaku just love kaadu… oka habit. Oka part of my life. ❤️


Part3

Situations Changed Everything ❤️‍🩹 Maa relationship happy ga continue avutunna time lo… thanaki home lo marriage ki pressure start ayyindi. Aa situation valla thanaki kuda chala tension perigindi. Oka side family pressure… inko side mana relationship. Daaniki madhyalo maa madhya kuda konni godavalu, misunderstandings vachayi. Mellaga chatting taggindi… calls taggayi… finally memu okariki okaram dooram aipoyam. Kani nijam cheppalante, memu dooram avvali ani eppudu anukoledu. Konni times love takkuva avvadam valla kaadu… situations valla people dooram avutaru. Alane maa story lo kuda jarigindi. Oka time lo roju maatladukunna memu… almost one year gap lo okariki okaram dooranga undipoyam.


Part 4

Almost one year gap tarvatha… tanu malli naatho matladatam start chesindi. Ee sari situation completely different ga undi. Paatavi tensions levu. Paatavi unnecessary godavalu levu. Just happy ga matladukuntunnam. Malli old connection vachinattu anipinchindi. Inka interesting thing enti ante… ee sari memu past gurinchi ekkuva think cheyyatly and future gurenche kuda, . Em jarugutundo manaki teliyadu. Future ela untundo kuda teliyadu. Kani oka thing matram clear… Mana happiness ni maname enduku lose cheskovali? Life lo manaki happiness iche person ni, konni problems or situations valla completely lose avvadam enduku? Anduke ee sari past lo jarigina vatini pakkana petti… present lo happy ga undali Anukuntunnam, future gurinche alochenchakunda velinanne memories create chysukovale anukuntunnam .
